Tenor needs one extra click

Tenor won’t let other websites read their pages, so the link from their share button doesn’t say where the actual GIF lives. Grabbing the GIF itself only takes a second:

  1. Open the GIF on tenor.com.
  2. Right-click it — on a phone, press and hold.
  3. Choose “Copy image address” (Safari says “Copy Image Link”).
  4. Paste it here. It’ll start with media.tenor.com.

✨ Don’t worry if you grab a small one — we swap in the biggest version Tenor has.

🍿 Or use a Giphy link instead. Those work as-is, no extra step.
