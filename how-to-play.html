HOW THE RAID GAME WORKS

🌱 Grow a little okra-hero in chat, gear it up, and march the whole patch against one big bad every week.
*all from chat while Nikki’s live · the battle plays out on the site

▶ ENTER THE RAID

🌿 THE WEEKLY LOOP

One harvest, start to finish — here’s the whole season-week in six sprouts.

  1. Sprout a hero 🌱 Subscribers plant an okra-hero with !create <class>. Your class picks your battlefield job (tank, healer, or damage) and you start with some humble starter gear.
  2. Chat to grow 💬 Every message you send while the stream is live sprinkles EXP on your hero. Hang out, yap in chat, and watch that bar fill.
  3. Level up & gear up ⚒️ Fill the EXP bar to level up and get stronger. When loot drops in chat, !grab to enter the draw, then !equip what you win into your weapon, armor, and trinket slots. Gear only works for its own role — if it isn’t for yours, hand it to someone it helps with !give.
  4. Muster the patch 📣 When the weekly boss is announced, a signup window opens. Subscribers type !muster to enlist your hero in this week’s fight. The roster fills up live on the Muster page.
  5. Raid night ⚔️ At raid night the roster locks and an automated, turn-by-turn battle plays out — your levels, gear, and team balance all decide how it goes. Nobody types during the fight; it resolves on its own.
  6. Watch, loot, climb 🏆 Watch the replay in the Arena and check where your damage landed on the Leaderboard. If the boss goes down, every hero on the roster gets a piece of gear — it lands straight in your !bag. Then it all begins again next week.

🎒 WHERE GEAR COMES FROM

Two ways, and they work differently. Everything you own lives in your !bag until you !equip it.

  1. Chat drops — a draw anyone can enter 🎲 An item drops into chat now and then while Nikki’s live, and whenever someone cheers bits. kennyBot announces it by name, everyone has 60 seconds to !grab, and when the window closes one entrant is drawn at random and gets it. Every entrant has identical odds — sub tier buys no loot luck. Bigger cheers can’t buy you the item, but they do raise how good it is for whoever wins it.
  2. Raid rewards — automatic, for showing up ⚔️ Clear the weekly boss and every hero on the roster gets one piece of gear, in their own role, straight into their bag. There is nothing to type and nothing to win — it happens when the battle resolves, whether you were watching or not. Surviving the fight improves what you can get, and the MVP gets the best odds of all. A wipe pays nothing.

💡 Browse everything that can drop in the Item Compendium — it lists which classes can wear each piece.

🛡️ GEAR ONLY WORKS FOR ITS OWN ROLE

The single most common mix-up — worth thirty seconds of your time.

  • Every item serves exactly one role. A tank’s breastplate does nothing for a healer, and a healer’s scepter does nothing for a damage-dealer. !equip will tell you rather than let you wear something useless.
  • Your class fixes your role. Guardian is the tank. Mender is the healer. Berserker, Arcanist and Ranger all deal damage, and share the same gear.
  • Off-role loot isn’t wasted — it’s currency. !bag marks anything you can’t use with ⛔. Pass it to someone who can with !give @user <#>, or swap for something you need with !trade @user <#>. The patch gears up faster when gear moves.

📖 CHAT COMMANDS

Type these in Nikki’s Twitch chat. Commands marked subs are subscriber-only.

  • !create <class> subs
    Plant your okra-hero. Pick one class: Guardian (tank), Mender (healer), Berserker, Arcanist, or Ranger (all damage). You get matching starter gear.
  • !char also: !me
    View your character — class, level, EXP, rating, combat stats, and what you’ve got equipped.
  • !bag also: !inventory, !inv
    Peek at your unequipped loot — everything you’ve grabbed but aren’t wearing yet. Anything your role can’t use is marked ⛔.
  • !equip <item>
    Wear an item from your bag — it slots into its weapon, armor, or trinket spot automatically. Gear for another role is refused (it would do nothing) and kennyBot tells you which class it’s for.
  • !unequip <slot|item>
    Bare a slot (weapon / armor / trinket) or a named item — it goes back into your bag.
  • !grab subs also: !loot
    Enter the drawing for the active loot drop while the window’s open. One winner is drawn when it closes — the item goes to them. 🎲
  • !give @user <item|#> also: !offer, !gift
    Hand an item (or credits) to someone else — they reply !offer accept to take it. The tidy way to move gear your role can’t use.
  • !trade @user <item|#>
    Propose a swap — they counter with what they’re putting up, then either side accepts or declines.
  • !muster subs to muster
    During the signup window, enlist your hero for this week’s raid (subscribers). Any time, it also reports the current raid status and links you to the action.
  • !top optional: !top damage
    See the season leaderboard — the patch’s top 5 hardest hitters.

🛠️ This page is about the raid game. kennyBot does plenty besides — clips, timers, fun facts, credits and the OKRAMARKET — and mods have a few behind-the-scenes commands for running the show. It’s all on the full command list.

🌟 GOOD TO KNOW

  • EXP only grows while live. Your hero earns EXP from chatting only when the channel is actually live — off-stream messages don’t count.
  • Leveling is steady, not lucky. Each level needs a fixed EXP bar to fill; once it’s full, your level-up lands within a few guaranteed messages. There’s no random jackpot and no bad-luck streak — fill the bar, get the level.
  • Higher sub tiers hit harder. A higher subscription tier boosts both how fast your hero earns EXP and its combat power on raid night.
  • Chat drops are a fair draw. Every entrant — and every subscriber tier — has the exact same odds of winning the drawing; no tier gets better loot luck than another.
  • Raid rewards need no luck at all. Clear the boss and everyone on the roster is paid, automatically, in their own role. Mustering is the whole requirement.
  • Bits make drops better, not likelier to be yours. A cheer of 500+ bits pops a drop for the whole chat, and the bigger the cheer the higher the guaranteed rarity floor. It’s still an open draw — the cheerer gets no edge.

❓ FAQ

I’m subscribed but I’m not gaining EXP — what gives?

EXP only accrues while the stream is live. If the channel is offline, your messages won’t feed your hero — pop back in once Nikki’s live. 🌱

I equipped something and my rating didn’t move. Why?

It was almost certainly gear for a different role — that’s worth nothing to you no matter how rare it is. !equip now refuses it outright and tells you which class it’s for, and !bag marks it ⛔. Pass it on with !give. 🎁

Something appeared in my bag and I never grabbed anything.

You were on the roster for a raid that won. Every hero who mustered gets one piece of gear when the boss goes down — no typing, no draw. Surviving the fight (and being MVP) improves what you can get. ⚔️

My sub lapsed. Did I lose my hero?

Nope! Your hero, level, and gear are all kept, and you keep earning EXP from chatting while live. You’ll just need to re-sub to create a new hero or to muster for a raid with !muster.
